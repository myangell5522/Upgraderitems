Item Upgrader adds a case-battle style gambling machine to Terraria.

Every item in the game - vanilla or modded - is given a market value derived from its own stats:
effective DPS, defense, tool power, rarity tier and vendor price. The value is shown at the bottom
of the item's tooltip next to an animated stack of flying money.

Stake an item, pick the item you want, and the wheel tells you your odds: your value divided by the
target's value. Win and the new item is yours. Lose and the stake is gone.

Features
- Upgrade by class: weapon into weapon, armor into armor, accessory into accessory.
- Full modded item support, values and all - nothing to configure.
- Searchable, sortable catalog with per-item odds.
- x2 / x4 / x8 and 30% / 50% / 70% shortcut buttons.
- Animated chance wheel that lands on the real roll.
- Win/loss stats, streaks and recent-result history.
- Favorited items are protected from being staked.
- Cash button in the inventory (right-drag to move it) and a keybind, default U.
- Client and server config: house edge, chance caps, failure refunds, cooldown and more.
